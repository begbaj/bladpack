# ⚔️ Blad Modpack (WIP)

> *Guys, I have an idea for the traditional two-week Minecraft server...*

Blad is a **technical, automation and adventure** modpack built around one idea: **vanilla mechanics should matter.**
No skipping the basics — you'll need them to progress. And it's harder to survive, too. No mega storage in the early game, no big-ass weapons after only 2 IRL days.

---

## 🚧 Work in Progress

Blad is still in **alpha**. Some claims (like the game being balanced) may not actually be true. We haven't tested this pack enough. Any suggestions are highly appreciated. Reach out to me on [GitHub](https://github.com/begbaj/bladpack).
Expect (not that) frequent updates, game-breaking changes, and mods coming and going. Back up your worlds together with the pack itself, since updates may break them!

---

## ✨ What to Expect

### ⚙️ Automation and Progression

Automate key processes and watch your base grow as you advance. Build technical setups with **Mekanism** and **Applied Energistics 2**, or grow your resources with the farming-centered **Mystical Agriculture**.

### 🗺️ Exploration and RPG

- A richer **Overworld, Nether and End**, with improved structure generation and new dimensions like **The Aether**
- Unique **artifacts, backpacks and treasures** waiting to be discovered
- Classes, spells, relics and skill trees from the **RPG Series**: become an archer, paladin, rogue or wizard
- **Dangerous mobs** that make every encounter count

### 🌦️ Survival That Fights Back

- **Thirst and temperature** to keep an eye on
- **Seasons** that change how and when you farm
- Survival mechanics that challenge your skills without turning into a chore

### 🔨 Rebalanced Crafting

Progression is built around crafting gatekeeping. We have Waystones, but they don't spawn that often, and to craft one you basically need to kill the Ender Dragon first. We have Traveler's Backpacks, but finding a mob wearing one is rare, and crafting one also requires killing the Ender Dragon. In vanilla Minecraft you gain access to portable storage with Shulker Boxes, and the same goes for Bladpack. This is what we mean by **vanilla mechanics should matter**.

Every tier feels earned, all the way to a rewarding endgame and post-endgame.
